1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

1.1 Problem Statement

Academic institutions collect extensive operational and academic data on students, including attendance records, weekly self-study hours, home internet connectivity, and continuous assessment examination scores. However, institutions frequently encounter operational bottlenecks:

  • Administrative datasets contain duplicates, missing values, and out-of-range clerical recording errors that compromise evaluation integrity.
  • Struggling students are often identified only after end-semester examinations, precluding timely remedial intervention.
  • Absence of empirical analysis linking attendance deficits and digital divide disparities to academic outcomes.

This project develops an automated, reproducible data analysis pipeline using Python, NumPy, and Pandas to clean, transform, explore, and report key performance trends, pinpoint at-risk students, and formulate 5 evidence-based academic support recommendations.

1.2 Core Project Objectives

  • Data Hygiene & Integrity: Ingest raw records, identify duplicate student identifiers, impute missing attendance values via department medians, and enforce $[0, 100]$ score bounds.
  • Feature Engineering: Calculate total marks, average marks, standard letter grades, and institutional attendance policy bands.
  • Comparative Analytics: Evaluate departmental performance differences and quantify the academic disparity caused by lack of internet access.
  • NumPy Statistical Exploration: Calculate median, standard deviation, quartiles (Q1, Q2, Q3), and detect score outliers using Tukey's Interquartile Range (IQR).
  • Intervention Planning: Formulate five actionable, empirical recommendations for academic leadership.

3. Functional Requirements (FR-01 through FR-15)

Each functional requirement maps directly to the 15 required tasks specified in the assignment curriculum:

Req ID Task Name Description & Technical Specification
FR-01 Load & Inspect Dataset Ingest CSV via pd.read_csv(); inspect .shape, .dtypes, .head(), and .describe().
FR-02 Missing & Duplicate Audit Report missing values with .isnull().sum(); identify and eliminate duplicate Student_IDs using drop_duplicates().
FR-03 Attendance Imputation Impute missing attendance using Department-wise Median (groupby('Department')['Attendance'].transform('median')); justify median robustness against outliers.
FR-04 Score Range Validation Audit all score columns for entries outside $[0, 100]$; rectify anomalies via boundary clipping (.clip(0, 100)).
FR-05 Total & Average Marks Compute Total_Marks as sum of 3 courses; calculate Average_Marks rounded to 2 decimal places.
FR-06 Categorical Grading Assign grades using pd.cut(): A ($85-100$), B ($70-85$), C ($55-70$), D ($40-55$), F ($<40$).
FR-07 Department Performance Aggregate marks and attendance across departments using groupby('Department').agg().
FR-08 Top 10 Rankers Extract top 10 academic achievers sorted by Average_Marks in descending order.
FR-09 At-Risk Cohort Identification Filter and report all students scoring below the passing threshold of $40\%$ for targeted intervention.
FR-10 Attendance Band Analytics Segment attendance into policy tiers ($<60\%$, $60-75\%$, $75-85\%$, $>85\%$) using pd.cut() and aggregate marks.
FR-11 Study Hours Correlation Calculate Pearson correlation between study hours and average marks (.corr()); group into study tiers.
FR-12 Digital Equity Analysis Compare performance of students with vs. without home internet access; quantify the achievement gap.
FR-13 NumPy Statistics Convert Python_Marks to NumPy array; compute mean, median, std dev, variance, and quartiles Q1, Q2, Q3.
FR-14 IQR Outlier Detection Calculate $\text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1$; establish fences $Q_1 - 1.5\text{IQR}$ and $Q_3 + 1.5\text{IQR}$; identify score outliers.
FR-15 Empirical Recommendations Synthesize 5 concrete, actionable academic support recommendations backed by calculated dataset findings.

7. Five Evidence-Based Academic Recommendations

  1. Automated Attendance Early Warning System: Trigger alerts at the $75\%$ attendance mark, addressing the observed drop to $45.59$ marks among students with $<60\%$ attendance.
  2. Dedicated Remedial Workshops: Provide weekend foundational tutoring for the 6 identified at-risk students scoring $<40\%$.
  3. Campus Digital Equity Initiative: Mitigate the observed $5.97$-mark deficit among students without home internet by offering 24/7 computer labs and offline materials.
  4. Structured Study Hours Programs: Encourage students with $<10$ study hrs/week to participate in peer study halls to work toward a healthy $15-20$ hours/week target ($r = 0.62$).
  5. Inter-Departmental Teaching Collaboration: Share active-learning and hands-on lab pedagogies across departments to elevate lower-scoring branches.
